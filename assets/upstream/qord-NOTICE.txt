qord
====

qord is a port of ord to the qbit chain.

Source: https://github.com/djh58/qord

qord is distributed under CC0-1.0. See LICENSE.


Upstream: ord
-------------

qord is derived from ord 0.29.1 by the Ord Maintainers and contributors
(https://github.com/ordinals/ord). ord is dedicated to the public domain under
CC0-1.0. qord keeps ord's Ordinal theory, inscription envelope, rune protocol,
explorer, API, and test work. CHANGELOG.md and the Git history record the
upstream contributors.

The qbit-specific changes in this repository are distributed under the same
CC0-1.0 terms. Those changes cover qbit chain parameters, block decoding,
addresses, P2MR and post-quantum signing, policy, wallet recovery, and
documentation.

bip.mediawiki is the Ordinal Numbers BIP draft by Casey Rodarmor, which is in
the public domain.

Third-party dependencies in release artifacts
---------------------------------------------

Release archives and container images include THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt and a
CycloneDX SBOM (qord.cdx.json). bin/generate-release-metadata generates both
from the locked Cargo dependency graph for each target. They include the
license texts for every compiled dependency, including:

* qbit-libbitcoinpqc 0.3.2 (https://github.com/Qbit-Org/qbit-libbitcoinpqc)
  License: MIT. Copyright (c) 2024 Hunter Beast @ Surmount Systems.

* The SPHINCS+ reference implementation vendored in qbit-libbitcoinpqc
  (https://github.com/sphincs/sphincsplus).
  License: (LicenseRef-SPHINCS-PLUS-Public-Domain OR CC0-1.0 OR 0BSD OR MIT-0)
  AND MIT.

release/qbit-pqc-license-inventory.json pins the vendored files and license
texts for those two components by SHA-256 hash.
